dZeroth Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

dZeroth ("dZeroth", "we", "us") is a text-first social app at dzeroth.com. This page explains what information we collect when you use it, why, where it's stored and how you can control it. We've tried to write it in plain language rather than legal boilerplate — if anything is unclear, email us.

Who we are

dZeroth is an early-stage, independently-run project built by a single founder based in India. There is no separate data protection team yet — privacy requests go straight to the founder at privacy@dzeroth.com.

Data we collect

  • Account data. When you sign up, Firebase Authentication (our identity provider) stores your email address and which sign-in method you used. If you sign up with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address from them.
  • Profile data. Your handle, display name, bio and avatar image, once you create a profile.
  • Posts and activity. As posting, media uploads, likes and follows ship, we'll store the posts you write, images you upload, and who you like and follow — all of this is planned but not all of it exists yet.
  • Technical data. Our servers keep operational logs that include a hashed version of your user ID (not your raw ID) for debugging and abuse prevention. Our app does not log your raw IP address. Google Cloud, which hosts our infrastructure, may process your IP address as part of its own network security and anti-abuse systems — we don't have access to raw IP logs ourselves.

Why we collect it

  • To create and secure your account, and verify it's really you.
  • To run the core features of the app: profiles, posts, follows, and showing you a timeline.
  • To keep the service safe — detecting spam, abuse and rate-limit violations.
  • To debug problems and keep the app reliable.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as responding to valid legal requests.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

We use Google Cloud and Firebase (Google LLC) to run dZeroth: hosting, the database, authentication, media storage and image moderation. They process data on our behalf as our infrastructure provider (a "processor" in privacy terminology) and don't use it for their own purposes. We don't share your data with any other company, and we don't sell it to anyone.

Where your data is stored

Your account and profile data is stored in Google Cloud's Mumbai (asia-south1) region. Images you upload are stored in the United States, because that's where Google Cloud's free storage tier is available. Both are protected by Google's standard encryption at rest.

How long we keep it

  • Account and profile data: for as long as your account exists.
  • Server logs: kept for about 30 days, then deleted.
  • Database backups: kept for up to 14 days, then rotated out.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it, or raise a grievance about how we've handled your data.

To do any of these, email privacy@dzeroth.com. Until in-app deletion and export tools ship, we handle these requests manually and will get back to you within 30 days.

Age requirement

dZeroth is for users aged 18 and over. We don't have a way to verify a parent or guardian's consent for younger users (as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act requires for anyone under 18), so we don't knowingly allow accounts for people under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, email us and we'll remove it.

Security

We rely on Firebase Authentication for sign-in and Google Cloud's infrastructure security, encryption at rest and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep access to production data restricted and monitor for unusual activity.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as dZeroth adds features (posts, media, likes, follows and beyond). We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. If a change is significant, we'll try to let you know in-app.

Contact

Questions, requests or complaints about your data: privacy@dzeroth.com.
