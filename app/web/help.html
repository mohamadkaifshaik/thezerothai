Your account#

How do I sign up or log in?

Use your email, or continue with Google or Apple where they are offered. A new email account has to verify its address before it can create a profile.

Is there an age limit?

Yes. Dzeroth is for adults aged 18 or older.

How do I report a post or a person?

Use Report on the post, message or profile. Reports are reviewed within 48 hours. See the community rules.

Your data#

How do I get a copy of my data?

Open Settings and choose Download my data, if it appears for your account. Otherwise email privacy@dzeroth.com from the address on your account.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings and choose Delete account, if it appears for your account, or email us the same way. The Privacy Policy lists what is deleted.

Location and plan areas#

Does Dzeroth read my location?

No. The app never asks your device for a location and stores no coordinates.

What is "my area"?

An optional area you choose in Settings > Location, from a fixed list of cities and neighbourhoods, so plans in or near it can be shown to you. You are asked to confirm you are 18 or older and to agree first.

Who can see it?

No other user. It is not on your profile.

How do I remove it?

Settings > Location > Clear my area. It is removed at once. We keep a record that you withdrew until you delete your account.

What about the area on a plan?

It belongs to the plan, not to you. Everyone who can see the plan sees it, and the plan appears in Nearby. Pick where the plan meets, never where you live. Edit or delete the plan to change or remove its area.

Where is this explained in full?

In the Privacy Policy.

Getting in touch#

Email privacy@dzeroth.com. Include the email address on your account so we can confirm it is you. We handle requests by hand and reply within 30 days.
