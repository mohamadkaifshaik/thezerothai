Signing up and signing in

Sign up or log in with your email, or continue with Google or Apple where offered. New email accounts must verify their email address before creating a profile. Dzeroth is for adults aged 18 or older.

Your data

If Download my data and Delete account appear in Settings you can do both yourself. Otherwise email privacy@dzeroth.com from the address on your account. See "Your rights" in the Privacy Policy.

Location and Plan areas

  • Does Dzeroth read my location? No. The app never asks your device for a location and stores no coordinates.
  • What is "my area"? An optional area you pick in Settings > Location, from a fixed list of cities and neighbourhoods, so Plans in or near it can be shown to you. You are asked to confirm you are 18 or older and to agree first.
  • Who can see my area? No other user. It is not on your profile.
  • How do I remove it? Settings > Location > Clear my area. It is removed at once. We keep a record that you withdrew until you delete your account.
  • What about a Plan's area? It belongs to the Plan, not to you. Everyone who can see the Plan sees it and it shows in Nearby. Pick where the Plan meets, never where you live. You can edit or delete the Plan to change or remove it.
