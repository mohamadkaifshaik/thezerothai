What it is

Dzeroth is a text-first social app: posts and replies, a timeline, direct messages, communities, lists and articles, and Plans, where you share something you want to do and people nearby can join. It is an early-stage, independently run project built by a single founder based in India, and it is meant for adults aged 18 or older.

Your place, your choice

Dzeroth never reads your phone's or browser's location. If you want to see Plans near you, you can pick one area from a fixed list of cities and neighbourhoods in Settings > Location. It is optional, other people can't see it, and you can clear it at any time. The area you give a Plan belongs to the Plan and is visible to everyone who can see the Plan.
