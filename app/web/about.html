About Dzeroth

A text-first social app for talking, and for making plans with people.

Dzeroth is a text-first social app. You can post and reply, follow people, message them privately, join communities, keep lists, write articles, and make plans.

Plans#

A plan is something you want to do, with a time and a place, that other people can ask to join. It is the part of Dzeroth we care about most: a way to go from a post to actually meeting someone.

Who builds it#

Dzeroth is an early-stage project built and run by one founder in India. That keeps it small and it means there is no one else between you and the person who answers your email. It is meant for adults aged 18 or older.

How it treats your data#

Dzeroth does not read your phone's or browser's location. If you want to see plans near you, you can pick one area from a fixed list of cities and neighbourhoods in Settings. It is optional, other people cannot see it, and you can clear it whenever you like. The area you give a plan belongs to the plan and is visible to everyone who can see the plan.

The Privacy Policy says exactly what we store, where, and for how long.

Contact#

Write to privacy@dzeroth.com about your account, your data or anything that looks wrong. The Terms and the community rules cover the rest.
